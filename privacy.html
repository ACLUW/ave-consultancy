Privacy & website information

Your information.
Your choice.

A straightforward explanation of what this website does with the information you enter.

Enquiries and matching

The capability navigator and enquiry builder run locally in your browser. They do not submit your text or contact details to AVE or another application server. The site does not save personal enquiry details in browser storage.

You can copy a brief, download it as a text file, or open a draft in your email application. Copying uses your device’s clipboard when available. Downloading creates a file on your device. Opening an email draft passes the brief to your email application; you decide whether to send it. Your email provider handles any message you send.

Service comparison

The site may use session storage to retain the service identifiers you select for comparison while you browse. These identifiers contain no personal enquiry information, remain in your browser and are cleared when the browser session ends. You can also clear the selection using the comparison controls. If session storage is unavailable, comparison still works on the current page.

Review checklist

The business confidence checklist is a local review aid. It resets when you leave the page and does not assert that any document has been supplied.

Hosting and assets

The site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP addresses under its hosting and privacy policies. There is no visitor analytics service, advertising tracker or embedded social feed on this site. Website fonts, photographs, scripts and styles are served with the site; no third-party font service is used.
