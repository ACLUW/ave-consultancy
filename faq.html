Ask AVE
Working with AVE
How does AVE’s delivery model work?

AVE is founder-led and project-based. It coordinates independent specialists, suppliers and service providers around an agreed scope, rather than operating five permanently staffed technical departments.

Getting started
How do I request a quotation?

Prepare an enquiry with the outcome you need, location, timeframe and any budget or participant details. AVE will discuss feasibility, provider requirements and the basis for a quotation. The website does not confirm bookings or prices.

Evidence
Can I request references or evidence of experience?

Ask for relevant client-authorised work records, dated photographs and references. Availability depends on the assignment, documentation and client consent. Named, independently verified case studies have not been supplied for this website.

Location
Where is AVE based?

AVE is based in Abuja, Nigeria. The location, provider availability and practical scope of each engagement are confirmed during consultation.

Privacy
What happens to information entered on this website?

The navigator and brief builder work in your browser. Personal enquiry details are not stored by this site or submitted to a server. You choose whether to copy, download or open an email draft.

Partnerships
How can a prospective partner or investor get in touch?

Use the partnership option on the enquiry page. Corporate records, financial information and provider agreements should be requested and reviewed separately. This website is an introduction, not an investment offer.

Property & projects
Can AVE help with materials sourcing?

Yes. An enquiry can cover sourcing, purchasing and delivery coordination. Specifications, supplier arrangements, budget and AVE’s responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

Logistics & concierge
Can you coordinate transport for a group?

Group transport can be discussed alongside routes, passenger numbers, dates and suitable vehicle providers. A booking is only confirmed under agreed terms.

Agro-allied operations
Can I discuss piglet and feed sourcing together?

Yes. Include quantities, location, preferred dates and the type of farm support needed. Availability and supplier arrangements require direct confirmation.

Business & digital
Can AVE prepare company profiles and presentations?

Corporate profiles, reports, invoices, presentations and promotional designs are included in the published scope. Content, formats, review rounds and delivery dates are agreed per assignment.

Education & elocution
Can coaching be tailored to my goals?

Yes. Start with the learning or speaking outcome you want, your current level and your preferred timetable. A suitable plan and session format can then be discussed.
