How we work

A flexible model.
A clear process.

Independent expertise works best with a shared direction. Our approach clarifies the outcome, the roles and the route to delivery.

01 / FROM BRIEF TO DELIVERY

Five stages.
One joined-up
conversation.

Each engagement is shaped by the work required, the available resources and the terms agreed with the client.

Tell us your requirement
01

Discover

Start with the outcome.

Discuss the requirement, location, constraints, timeline and what a useful result looks like.

02

Define the scope

Make responsibilities clear.

Agree deliverables and distinguish AVE’s coordination tasks from independent specialist or supplier responsibilities.

03

Mobilise

Bring the right resources together.

Confirm suitable providers, competence, availability and commercial arrangements for the agreed requirement.

04

Coordinate delivery

Keep the moving parts connected.

Coordinate activities, communicate progress, monitor relevant milestones and address changes or issues.

05

Close & learn

Finish with clarity.

Confirm delivery, record agreed outcomes, resolve outstanding items and invite feedback.

02 / WHO DOES WHAT

Connected delivery.
Clear responsibilities.

AVE / coordinating contact

Requirement discovery, scope coordination, client communication, practical oversight and issue escalation.

Independent specialists & suppliers

Agreed technical, supply or service tasks, with relevant competence, authorisation and availability.

You / the client

Define priorities, approve scope and commercial terms, provide necessary information and review agreed milestones.

Scope-specific technical or regulated work is handled through appropriately authorised providers. Contacts and networks do not imply public-sector endorsement or special agency powers.

03 / BEFORE WORK BEGINS

Agree the important
details first.

A clear written scope is the foundation of a useful engagement.

  • What is being delivered and what is outside scope
  • AVE’s responsibilities and any third-party contributions
  • Milestones, communication and approval arrangements
  • Pricing, payment terms and handling changes
  • Delivery confirmation and outstanding issues
