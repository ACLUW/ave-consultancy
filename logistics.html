Keep people, plans and resources moving.

Travel and operational arrangements have many moving parts. AVE brings suitable providers and practical scheduling together around the requirements of your engagement.

THE SCOPE

Where we can
support you.

Executive transport, group movement, travel and event logistics coordinated around your plans.

  • Executive and group transport coordination, including vehicle hire arrangements
  • Travel planning, accommodation bookings and itinerary management
  • Event-related logistics and operational scheduling
  • Vehicle sourcing and sales facilitation through suitable providers
  • Administrative concierge and on-the-ground coordination

Availability, routes, pricing and provider terms are confirmed per engagement. Any security-related needs require lawful arrangements through authorised providers; personal networks do not imply agency affiliation or official endorsement.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you enquire.

More questions, answered
Can you coordinate transport for a group?

Group transport can be discussed alongside routes, passenger numbers, dates and suitable vehicle providers. A booking is only confirmed under agreed terms.

Are travel and accommodation costs fixed?

No fixed tariff is published. Costs depend on dates, locations, availability and provider terms, and are confirmed through a scoped quotation.
