03 / Agro-Allied Operations

Practical connections across livestock and supply.

AVE’s agro-allied offering brings practical operating experience and supplier relationships to livestock, feed and farm-related requirements. Each commitment depends on resources and arrangements confirmed for the engagement.

Discuss this serviceAll capabilities

THE SCOPE

Where we can
support you.

Livestock activity, sourcing and agricultural coordination shaped around the actual requirement.

  • Commercial livestock and pig-rearing activity under agreed direct or farm-provider arrangements
  • Piglet sourcing, livestock sales and boarding coordination
  • Feed sourcing, mixing arrangements and supply coordination
  • Communication with farmers, suppliers and relevant animal-health professionals

Scope must account for animal welfare, disease control, feed quality and competent veterinary support. Supply, boarding and farm-provider arrangements are confirmed individually.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you enquire.

More questions, answered
Can I discuss piglet and feed sourcing together?

Yes. Include quantities, location, preferred dates and the type of farm support needed. Availability and supplier arrangements require direct confirmation.

How are animal-health needs handled?

Veterinary and other specialist needs must be addressed by competent professionals under the agreed operating arrangements. The navigator does not provide animal-health advice.
