THE SCOPE

Where we can
support you.

Property facilitation, site coordination and materials sourcing, with one clear point of contact.

  • Property sourcing, acquisition, sales and leasing facilitation
  • Construction site supervision and coordination of trades within an agreed scope
  • Building material sourcing, purchasing and delivery coordination
  • Budget-oriented quantity estimation and procurement administration
  • Coordination with appropriately qualified design, engineering and interior specialists

AVE’s role is coordination and agreed site oversight. Technical design, engineering certification, surveying and other reserved professional work remain with suitably authorised practitioners.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you enquire.

More questions, answered
Can AVE help with materials sourcing?

Yes. An enquiry can cover sourcing, purchasing and delivery coordination. Specifications, supplier arrangements, budget and AVE’s responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

Does AVE provide engineering certification?

Reserved technical work is handled by suitably authorised practitioners. AVE’s coordination role does not itself imply engineering or surveying authorisation.
